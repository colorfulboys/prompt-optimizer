❓ FAQ

Q1: Format is messed up after conversion?

Cause: Original PDF had complex layout (multi-column, nested tables).

Fix: JianHeBox offers "Academic/Publication mode" for smart layout detection. Manual touch-up for complex PDFs.

Q2: Chinese garbled?

Cause: PDF doesn't have embedded fonts.

Fix: Use Adobe Acrobat to re-export PDF with all fonts embedded, then convert.

Q3: Scanned PDF is empty?

Cause: OCR not enabled.

Fix: JianHeBox has OCR mode. Choose language: Chinese + English.

Q4: Images lost?

Fix: JianHeBox extracts images by default. Check "Keep Images" option.
